What are Pebble Pals?

At Pebble Pals, we believe everyone deserves a companion who’s rock solid — literally. Our Pebble Pals are low-maintenance friends with big personalities and even bigger hearts (metaphorically, of course). They don’t shed, bark, or need walks — just a little shelf space and your undivided admiration. Whether you’re looking for a study buddy, a desk decoration, or a lifelong sediment of happiness, your perfect Pebble Pal is waiting to roll into your life.
